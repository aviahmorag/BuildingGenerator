Building Generator

Transform facade images into 3D Blender buildings with textures

1. Upload Image
2. Rectify (Optional)
3. Generate Building

Step 1: Upload your facade image

  1. Choose an image of a building facade
  2. The image will be used as texture for all 4 sides of the building
  3. Works best with rectangular facade images

📁 Drop your facade image here

or click to browse (PNG, JPG, JPEG)

Step 2: Rectify your image (Optional)

  1. If your facade is not rectangular, you can transform it
  2. Click on 4 corners of the facade
  3. Transform it to a perfect rectangle
  4. Or skip this step if your image is already rectangular

Step 3: Generate your 3D building

  1. Set the building dimensions
  2. Click "Generate 3D Building"
  3. Two files will be saved to your Downloads folder:
    • A .blend file (3D model for Blender)
    • A .png texture file (facade image)
